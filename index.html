Key Insights

  • Short Term (0-5 Yrs): Heavy capital investment in tech infrastructure (DPIIC, GPU facility) and completion of National Geophysical Mapping.
  • Medium Term (0-10 Yrs): Shift towards predictive mapping algorithms and expanding landslide forecasting to 80 districts.
  • Long Term (2047): The ultimate goal is the "3D/4D Map of India" and deep lithosphere probing (IndPROBE).

Energy Transition & Security

  • Lithium & Cobalt: Aggressive exploration required in pegmatite belts.
  • Natural Hydrogen: New exploration for 'White Hydrogen' reservoirs.
  • Coal Innovation: Research on Coal-to-Liquid (CTL) and coal-derived anodes for Li-ion batteries.

Advanced Mapping Tech

  • Deep Seated: Utilization of 2-D Seismic & Magnetotelluric (MT) surveys.
  • Offshore: Upgrading Marine Gravimeters & ROVs for coastal surveys.
  • Subsurface Map: Creation of a definitive "Subsurface Critical Mineral Map" of India by 2039.
